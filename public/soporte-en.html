Frequently asked questions

How do I invite a brother?

An administrator opens the List of brothers, opens the brother's record and taps Invite. The app creates two messages: one with the link and one with the code, so it is easy to copy and paste.

I changed phones or lost mine. How do I recover my account?

  • If you linked your email to your account (Settings → My account), open the app and tap “Create account or sign in as administrator”. Enter your email, you receive a link, and you get back in with everything as you left it.
  • If you didn't (brothers don't need an email), an administrator has to send you a new invitation: open your record in the List of brothers and tap Invite. It is quick, and you get your place back with everything in the congregation. The only thing lost is your Report, if you had no backup (Settings → Backup).

How do I link my email?

It is optional and meant for administrators, so they don't lose access if they change phones: Settings → My account → Link my email. You will receive a confirmation link; open it from the same device. Other brothers don't need any email.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In Settings → My account → Delete my account. Your account, your record and the data stored on your device are deleted. If you are the only administrator of a congregation with other brothers, you must first name another one. More details in the privacy policy.

I'm not receiving notifications

  • Check that they are allowed: device Settings → Notifications → Wkit.
  • Open Wkit with your account at least once, so the device is registered.
  • A single device receives the notices of one person at a time: whoever is signed in.

I don't see what another brother changed

Pull down on the screen to refresh, or reopen the app.

How do I change the language?

Wkit uses your device's language. To change it only for Wkit: device Settings → Apps → Wkit → Language. The web panel detects your browser's language and you can change it with the drop-down at the top right.

I want to change or correct my data

The record and the schedule are managed by your congregation's administrators. Ask them to correct it; if you need anything else, write to us.
