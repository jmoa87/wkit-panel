Delete your Wkit account

Wkit · Last updated: October 20, 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Wkit app and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account from the app

  1. Open Wkit and go to Menu → Settings.
  2. Tap My account.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.
Settings → My account → Delete my account.

If you can no longer open the app

Write to titanestudiosorg@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account (administrators), or tell us the name of your congregation and your full name (other members), and we will delete your account and data. A congregation administrator can also delete your record from the list of brothers.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (and its email address, if you gave one) and your record in the congregation's list of brothers.
  • Any territories assigned to you are freed up.
  • The data stored on your device, including your service Report, is erased.
  • If you are the only person in your congregation, the congregation is also deleted with all its data (we ask you expressly before doing so).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Announcements, schedules and other content that belongs to the congregation are managed by its administrators and remain in the congregation after your record is removed. The congregation's administrators can delete them.
  • Data that no longer exists may remain for a limited time in our database provider's backups before it is erased completely.
  • We do not keep your personal data for any other purpose.

Subscriptions

Deleting the account does not cancel a paid subscription, if there is one. Cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions (Android) or in your Apple ID settings (iPhone).
